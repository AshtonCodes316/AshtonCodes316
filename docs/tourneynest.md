## Problem and motivation

Tournament weekends combine schedules, packing, travel, reminders, and privacy-sensitive family coordination.

## Design and engineering focus

A documented Schedule → Review → Prepare → Pack → Leave → Play → Reset → Remember workflow; architecture discussions include SwiftUI, backend data modeling, and privacy boundaries.

Structural checkpoints are documented; native iOS device/simulator validation and some Apple integrations remain deferred.
