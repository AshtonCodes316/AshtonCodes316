# Interview conversion kit — AI implementation and technical customer success

**Use:** Tailor each application to the actual listing. These are project-based examples, not claims of paid client work or live AI integrations. Confirm personal employment dates, education, tools, and metrics against your own records before submitting.

## 8-minute interview demo

| Time | Demonstrate | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–1:00 | Portfolio and one-page profile | My background is in customer-facing operations; this portfolio documents the technical work I'm building toward. |
| 1:00–3:00 | Implementation Dashboard | Walk through a missing milestone, blocker, owner, and simulated go-live gate. |
| 3:00–5:00 | Workflow Studio or Automation Lab | Show an unmet prerequisite or denied approval and explain the next action. |
| 5:00–6:30 | Case study and test evidence | Explain what was tested and how someone can reproduce the result. |
| 6:30–8:00 | Limits and role fit | These are fictional browser demos, not customer deployments or live AI. I want to apply this process discipline on a real team. |

## Five questions to rehearse

1. **Why implementation?** Connect business operations, requirement clarity, and handoffs to the dashboard example.
2. **How do you handle a blocker?** Describe identifying the missing prerequisite, naming an owner, communicating status, and verifying resolution.
3. **What happens if approval is denied?** Demonstrate the Control Room rejection path; explain that the demo is deterministic.
4. **How do you know something works?** Show the scenario tests, reproducible checks, and documented limitations; don't substitute green CI for production proof.
5. **What haven't you done yet?** Be clear about no verified production AI integrations or customer deployments from these demos; describe what you would learn with a team.

## Application quality gate

- [ ] Confirm the role is open and genuinely remote in your eligible location.
- [ ] Check experience and degree requirements before investing time.
- [ ] Tailor summary and top three bullets to the role; don't fabricate tools, outcomes, or years.
- [ ] Use one primary demo and one relevant case study, not a wall of links.
- [ ] Record the application and follow-up date only after submission.
- [ ] Obtain approval before sending messages or submitting applications.
