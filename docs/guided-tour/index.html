A self-paced walkthrough for recruiters and technical hiring managers. The suggested timing is illustrative, not a recorded video or a measured completion time.

00–15s · Problem

Automation must handle ambiguous requests, customer handoffs, and consequential actions without silently gaining authority.

15–30s · Architecture

Inspect the six-stage Orchestration Atlas and its human-approval boundary.

30–45s · Demo

Choose a fictional business scenario in the Control Room and step through a workflow.

45–60s · Failure

Run a stale approval or mixed-authority scenario in the Failure Lab. The reference policy should refuse it.

60–75s · Evidence

Open the Evidence Center, verify an exact CI run, and inspect reproduction steps.

75–90s · Connect

Review the one-page profile or connect on LinkedIn.
